About

A Marine gunsmith and a business manager. South Lamar County.

The name

Some would say Bastard is a bad word. The unit Alex served with, 2nd Bn 4th Marines, nickname is the Magnificent Bastards. The business name is a salute to those lost in Afghanistan in 2011, and to Marines who have fought and died under the battalion colors since 1914.

Alex Rivers

Owner · Gunsmith · Type 07 FFL

8-year USMC veteran, 2/4 Marines (Magnificent Bastards). Certified gunsmith. Poultry farmer in south Lamar County. Husband, father, avid shooter.
